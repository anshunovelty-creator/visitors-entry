2. Visitor check-in flow

Visitor check-in flow, phone-first

Paper & Ink at phone size. Each screen also scales to a tablet; on a landscape tablet the heading moves left and the actions move right, like the screen you picked. Real names are placeholders.

Desk device (phone or tablet)Check-in takes effect right away and the host is emailed immediately. It can scan invite QR codes with its camera.
Visitor's own phoneOpened from the poster QR or the link in the invite email. The visit waits as "Arriving" until reception confirms, and only then is the host emailed.
Same screens on bothOne set of screens. Only the first action differs: the desk device scans the invite QR, while a visitor's own phone has them type the code.
