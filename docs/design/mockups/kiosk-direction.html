Novelty Labels visitor system: design mockups

1. Visual direction

Lobby kiosk: which visual direction feels like Novelty Labels?

Same welcome screen, same three choices, drawn three ways, shown as a landscape tablet. Whichever you pick sets the look for the kiosk, the reception console, the host portal and the emails. Typography, spacing and details get refined after this.

Decision: A, Paper & Ink, designed mobile-first so it works on phones and tablets.

A

Paper & Ink

Calm and corporate: light background, your green as the ink, a barcode accent. The quietest of the three.
